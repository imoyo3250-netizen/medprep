1. Upload both PDFs to Examinations

Upload the questions PDF and the separate answer-key PDF. The importer extracts selectable text in your browser, parses common numbered MCQs, and lets you review everything before saving. Scanned/image-only PDFs need OCR and are not supported by this version.

2. Review imported questions and answers

Check every question, option and answer carefully. Supported question layout: numbered questions such as “1. Question text”, with options such as “A. Option text” on separate lines. Supported answer layout: “1. A”, “1) A”, or “1 - A”. Correct any parsing errors before saving.

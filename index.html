📚 HOW TO USE THE TESTS

1. Tests labelled “FROM THESE”
Tests labelled FROM THESE BOOK are designed to ensure that you know and understand the information from your textbooks,they measure book knowlegde not exam knowledge.

2. Tests labelled “Cell 1” or “Embryology”
Tests labelled Cell 1 or Embryology are based on question papers. They are intended to help you practise examination-style questions.

3. Tests labelled “Revision ”
Tests labelled Revision are compilations of both the textbook-based tests and the question-paper-based tests.

4. Clinical Correlations
Clinical Correlations contain images and explanations to help you understand the concepts and connect your basic medical knowledge with clinical situations.

⚠️ IMPORTANT — ANSWER VERIFICATION

The answers on these pages have been thoroughly reviewed using Ganong, Snell, Junqueira's Basic Histology and relevant Google Scholar literature.

However, there may still be a few wrong answers. When in doubt, your own research and verification using your current textbook or other authoritative medical sources is advised.

If you see an answer that appears to be wrong, please contact/report it so it can be investigated and corrected.

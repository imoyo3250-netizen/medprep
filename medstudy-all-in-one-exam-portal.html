2. Examination rules

  1. Do not switch tabs, open another tab, minimize or close the examination page, or use screen sharing/another screen for unauthorized help.
  2. Close other browser tabs and unnecessary applications before starting. Do not use unauthorized notes, AI tools, messaging, websites, or assistance from another person.
  3. Ensure your device is charged. Do not lock, restart, shut down, or intentionally interrupt it during the exam.
  4. The browser may detect page hiding, fullscreen exits, and some window changes. These signals are imperfect; supported events can be logged for review.
  5. The page will request a screen-awake lock. If it cannot be enabled or is released, a warning will appear. Resolve this before starting where possible.
  6. Follow the invigilator's instructions. Suspected misconduct may be reviewed under the institution's examination rules.
  7. Intended marking rule: −1 mark for each wrong answer. Unanswered questions and total marks must follow the published exam settings.
